Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026 · Last updated: 12 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DALMIREN AI (“the App”) collects, uses, stores and shares information. The App is operated by Darlington Chidera Mbawike, trading as DARCHUMS (“we”, “us” or “our”), in South Africa.

Contact: darlingtonmbawike@gmail.com · +27 69 832 4267

Who the App is for: DALMIREN AI is designed for adults acting as parents, guardians, caregivers or educators. It provides guidance about children but is not intended for children to create accounts or use independently. Adults should not enter information they do not have authority to provide.

1. Information we process

Depending on the features you use and the information you choose to provide, the App may process:

  • Account information: name, email address, password verifier (the password itself is not stored in plain text), account identifiers and authentication/session tokens.
  • Family and child-profile information: names or labels, age or age group, family relationships, developmental context and other details an adult chooses to enter about a child or family situation.
  • Conversations and AI inputs: questions, prompts, messages, conversation history, context and AI-generated responses.
  • Memory and guidance information: saved memories, parenting goals, saved parenting plans, progress notes, follow-up records and specialist guidance sessions.
  • Privacy and consent records: choices about cloud processing and related consent records.
  • Subscription and purchase information: subscription status, entitlements and purchase-verification references. Google Play handles payment-card processing; the App does not need your full payment-card number.
  • Technical and security information: information generated by the service and its hosting infrastructure, such as request, error and security logs, which may include timestamps and network information.

Please avoid entering unnecessary sensitive information, including medical records, precise addresses, identity numbers or details about other people that are not needed for your request.

2. How we use information

We use information to:

  • create and manage accounts, authenticate users and protect the service;
  • provide parenting conversations, specialist guidance, memory, goals, plans and follow-up features;
  • personalise responses using information and context associated with your account;
  • record and enforce your cloud-processing consent choice;
  • manage subscriptions, entitlements and purchase verification;
  • maintain, troubleshoot, secure and improve the App; and
  • respond to support requests and comply with legal obligations.

3. AI processing and cloud consent

DALMIREN AI uses AI services to generate conversational and specialist guidance. Depending on the deployed configuration, requests may be processed by a locally hosted AI service or by OpenAI through its API.

Cloud processing is controlled by your explicit consent. When cloud-processing consent has not been granted, the backend is designed to disallow cloud processing. If a local provider is unavailable and cloud consent has not been granted, the request may fail rather than being sent to a cloud provider without consent.

If you grant cloud-processing consent and a request is routed to OpenAI, information needed to answer may be sent to OpenAI. This can include your current prompt, relevant conversation history, system instructions, saved-memory context and relevant child or family context. Some structured guidance workflows may also send an AI response for further structuring. OpenAI processes information under its own service terms and privacy documentation. Review OpenAI's Privacy Policy and OpenAI policies.

You can change your cloud-processing consent in the App's privacy or consent controls where available. Changing consent affects future processing; it does not undo processing that already occurred.

4. Who may receive information

We do not sell personal information. We share information only as needed for the purposes described in this Policy, including with:

  • Hosting and infrastructure providers: Render hosts the App's backend and related service infrastructure.
  • AI service providers: OpenAI may receive prompt and context information when cloud processing is consented to and used. A locally hosted AI provider may process requests in deployments configured to use one.
  • Google Play: Google Play provides app distribution and subscription/payment services. Purchase-verification information may be processed to validate entitlements.
  • Professional advisers, authorities or other parties: where reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights and safety, or respond to lawful requests.

Service providers process information to provide their services under their applicable terms. Their infrastructure may be located outside South Africa. Where cross-border processing occurs, we take steps intended to address applicable data-protection requirements.

5. Children's information

The App is intended for adults, not for direct use by children. An adult may choose to create a child profile or include information about a child to receive parenting guidance. The adult is responsible for ensuring they have the authority to provide that information and should provide only what is necessary. We do not ask children to create accounts or submit information directly.

If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, contact us so we can assess the request and take appropriate steps.

6. Retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App, maintain account and security records, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations. We do not currently publish a fixed automatic deletion period for every category of information.

Some App features delete data differently. For example, deleting a saved parenting plan removes that plan and its associated progress history from the account database. Deleting or deactivating a saved memory may mark it inactive rather than immediately physically erasing every associated database or backup copy. Clearing local App preferences does not delete your server account, conversations, child profiles or server-side memories.

You can request account deletion using the instructions on our Account Deletion page. Account deletion is intended to remove the account and associated personal records from the active application database. Limited records may be retained where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention or transaction-record purposes. Backup copies may persist for a limited period before being overwritten or removed through normal backup processes.

Deleting your DALMIREN AI account does not automatically cancel a subscription purchased through Google Play. You must cancel that subscription separately in Google Play.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information. The App's backend uses password hashing and authenticated access controls, and App-to-server communication uses HTTPS. No internet service or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We do not represent that the App uses end-to-end encryption.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, including South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, to object to certain processing, or to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. These rights may be subject to lawful limitations and record-retention requirements.

To make a privacy request, email darlingtonmbawike@gmail.com. Include enough information for us to identify your account and understand your request. Do not email your password or payment-card details. We may need to verify your identity before acting.
