Account deletion

How to delete your DALMIREN AI account and request deletion of associated personal information.

DALMIREN AI is operated by Darlington Chidera Mbawike, trading as DARCHUMS. You can request deletion of your DALMIREN AI account and associated personal information whether or not you can currently sign in to the App.

Option 1: Delete your account in the App

  1. Open DALMIREN AI and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings and choose Delete Account.
  3. Follow the confirmation steps, including re-entering your account password and typing the requested confirmation phrase if prompted.
  4. Submit the request and wait for the App to confirm that the server completed deletion.

If the in-app deletion option is unavailable, you cannot authenticate, or your account was created without a password, use the email request option below.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

Send an email to darlingtonmbawike@gmail.com with the subject DALMIREN AI Account Deletion Request.

Include:

  • the email address registered to your DALMIREN AI account;
  • a statement that you want your DALMIREN AI account deleted; and
  • any information reasonably needed to help us locate the account.
Do not send your password, one-time codes, full payment-card number or other authentication secrets by email. We may ask for reasonable proof of account ownership before processing a request.

We will review the request, verify account ownership where necessary, and process it or explain any lawful reason we cannot complete it. If you cannot access the registered email address, explain this in your request so we can discuss an alternative verification method.

What deletion affects

When an account deletion request is successfully processed, we intend to delete the account and associated personal records from the active application database, including account profile information, child profiles, conversations and messages, saved memories, parenting goals and plans, guidance and follow-up records, consent records, authentication tokens, and subscription-entitlement records.

Information that may remain

  • Limited transaction, security, fraud-prevention or audit information may be retained where required or permitted by law or needed to protect the service.
  • Some memory records may have been deactivated rather than physically erased before account deletion; deletion processing is intended to remove account-associated records from the active application database.
  • Backup copies may persist for a limited period before being overwritten or removed through normal backup processes.

Google Play subscriptions

Deleting your DALMIREN AI account does not automatically cancel a subscription purchased through Google Play. To avoid future subscription charges, open Google Play, go to Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, select DALMIREN AI and cancel the subscription separately. Your subscription remains subject to Google Play's cancellation and billing rules.
